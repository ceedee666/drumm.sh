## Abstract

The use of renewable energy is continuously increasing. As a result, accurate
forecasting of photovoltaic power generation is becoming more important for
energy suppliers and grid operators. Reliable day-ahead forecasts are
essential for electricity trading and stable grid operation.

The aim of this bachelor's thesis is to develop a prototype forecasting
service for day-ahead photovoltaic power generation using the SAP HANA PAL.
Historical photovoltaic generation data and weather data were collected,
processed and integrated into an SAP HANA Cloud environment.

Different forecasting methods were reviewed, and the Hybrid Gradient Boosting
Tree (HGBT) algorithm was selected for the implementation. Forecast accuracy
was evaluated using the metrics MAE, RMSE, nMAE and nRMSE. In addition, a
forecasting service was developed to automatically prepare the data, execute
the model and provide the forecasting results within the SAP HANA platform.

The results indicate that the developed forecasting service provides a
suitable basis for data-driven PV forecasting within an SAP-based system
environment.
